About Me

I’m Chinwendu Emmanuel Onukwuli, founder and lead creative director of RokcityDesigns. I’m a graphic designer, philosopher, gospel music minister and social commentator. I advocate for youth inclusion in politics and help young people develop graphic design skills through RokcityDesigns.

My design work spans church events, social campaigns and promotions for fashion, beauty and food businesses.

Design services

  • Brand identity

    Visual direction, colours and typography for your brand.

  • Social media design

    Graphics for announcements, campaigns and recurring posts.

  • Flyer design

    Event, church and promotional flyers for print or digital use.

  • Logo & 3D mockups

    Logo design and mockups that show how it looks in context.

  • Business advertising

    Ad graphics that make offers and products easy to understand.
